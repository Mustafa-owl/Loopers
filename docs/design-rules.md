# Design rules

These come from the owner's feedback on earlier versions. Check every new
page or change against them before it ships.

## Colour
- The brand: deep service blue (`--brand`) for the top bar and main
  buttons, with a thin stripe in the services' colours under it. Electricity
  is bright blue (`--c-power`), water is teal (`--c-water`), everywhere.
  Surfaces are white and light grey, text near-black.
- No warm paper or cream backgrounds. No orange, amber or terracotta
  accents. No decorative green.
- Red only means "no power / no water". Green only means "fixed / back to
  normal". Neither is used for decoration or emphasis.
- Never put coloured bold text next to more coloured bold text. Emphasis
  comes from weight and size, in the ink colour.

## Type
- Brand fonts: Barlow for Latin text (English, Kurmanji), Barlow Semi Condensed bold for headings and figures.
- One Arabic-script face for Sorani and Arabic, headings and text alike
  (Noto Sans Arabic, the standard on phones). Never mix it with a second Arabic-script
  typeface such as a Kufi.
- Sorani and Arabic get more line height than English (about 1.6 to 1.75
  for text, 1.45 for headings) and never any letter spacing.

## Shapes
- No cards for the sake of cards: no rounded grey boxes around content
  that reads fine on the page.
- No pills, chips or badges for plain words ("Yours", a status).
- No coloured dots or squares used as legends next to a word.
- Buttons are the only rounded things; secondary buttons have a thin edge.

## Text
- Sentence case. No all-caps labels, no small label above every heading.
- Parts of a line are joined with commas (Arabic comma in Sorani and
  Arabic), never with middle dots or bullets.
- Write what's true and specific to Erbil and this site. Every number on a
  page must come from the code or a cited source.

## Process
- Screenshot every changed page in English and Sorani, light and dark,
  desktop and phone, and look at them before saying it's done.
- Run the accessibility check (no WCAG A/AA failures) and the tests.
